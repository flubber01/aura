# AURA OS Collaboration & Monetization — Areas 96 and 99

## Product tiers

AURA OS now treats its commercial structure as three product tiers plus a separately reviewed royalty path. This is a **local product catalog**, not a payment processor, invoice, subscription system or contract automation engine.

“Active” cannot be faked by a local price intent. Until an official future billing/contract operation creates a verified entitlement, the product stays in its local Base/trial policy.

## Branding and notice policy

- **Base and restricted trial:** generated export/render briefs carry `AURA_BASE_WATERMARK_REQUIRED_IN_EXPORT_BRIEFS`. AURA does not claim it has overlaid a watermark into a real external render when no renderer is connected.
- **Pro / Epic:** watermark and AURA-house-promotion policy are `NOT_REQUIRED_WHILE_ENTITLED` only while a verified active entitlement exists.
- **Royalty:** a contract intent is not a signed contract. It remains `ROYALTY_CONTRACT_REVIEW_REQUIRED` until human/legal/business evidence is recorded through a future production contract operation.
- **Fair frequency:** eligible Base/trial UI can display a small, honest AURA Creator Notice at most once every 28 minutes. It is dismissible, does not interrupt forms, recording, review, active creative work or the underlying workflow, and does not masquerade as an external ad.

The point is sustainable product monetization without sabotaging the creator's concentration or pretending an unconnected biller has charged anyone.

## Collaboration Room — Area 99

`/creator-collaboration` provides a self-hosted local-first room for a creator and trusted collaborator in another city.

It includes:

- content sprint, release room, product drop, brand world and time-off coverage modes;
- a documented rights/privacy boundary per room;
- one-time-issued participant room codes, stored hashed in the local store;
- local messages/ideas, bound to participant code;
- deterministic AURA Content Bot facilitation that converts room notes into `PENDING_OWNER_REVIEW` cards;
- handoff path into Area 96 Content Missions, Area 97 Asset Reactor/Continuity and Area 98 Remote Approval;
- connector-gate catalog for self-hosted web, Telegram/BotFather, WhatsApp Cloud and Discord/Slack/Matrix.

## Remote access truth

A room's local API can be used on the self-hosted AURA service. It is **not** a production identity provider, secure public chat deployment or automatic multi-city transport claim. Before real remote collaboration, the deployment owner must deliberately add real TLS, authentication and network controls (for example a VPN/reverse proxy/SSO architecture) or use a verified official bridge adapter.

Telegram Area 78 and Area 98 already provide the owner-review transport path. WhatsApp Cloud remains an official Meta setup gate requiring business/app verification, secret storage outside AURA, webhook validation, opt-in/template/privacy handling and a supported documented message/group capability.

No external messenger group, message, participant identity, token or credential is created, read or sent by Area 99.
