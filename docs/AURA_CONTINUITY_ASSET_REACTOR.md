# AURA OS Creator Continuity & Asset Reactor — Area 97

## The creator problem it addresses

Creators do not stop being parents, partners, carers or humans when their content cycle is active. A period away can create a real operational gap: no prepared formats, a fragmented handover, stale offers, no return plan and pressure to be permanently online.

Area 97 makes that gap a **controlled local coverage workflow**. It is not a claim that a creator can be replaced, nor a promise of artificial reach. The goal is simpler: prepare real owner-approved material before a pause, preserve a coherent public rhythm where legitimate/appropriate, and make return-to-work deliberate instead of chaotic.

## What the Continuity Engine does

1. **Creator identity settings**
   - Declares whether coverage is text/existing media only, an owned-avatar render brief, or a future official avatar-provider gate.
   - Captures an owner-provided avatar/figure reference and an explicit rights/consent statement when an avatar is used.
   - Supports text-only, local browser system voice, or a future authorized-own-voice-provider setting.
   - A future own-voice provider requires a matching Area-96 live-consent attestation. That attestation stores no audio and is not biometric/liveness verification.

2. **Time-off coverage plan**
   - Models vacation/family time, care/health/private time, studio focus, planned batch, or a launch buffer.
   - Stores a private boundary, own content bank, date range, cadence and intended AURA lanes.
   - Keeps every plan as local drafts until an official account/connector and final review exist.

3. **Coverage preload and draft cycles**
   - Activation creates preloaded local coverage cards for the absence window.
   - Cards include a reviewable script, avatar/voice treatment, derivative/caption intentions and per-lane `NOT_PUBLISHED` gates.
   - An additional local draft-cycle endpoint supports an intentionally installed self-hosted runner / cron tick later. It has no external execution path.

4. **Human comment and return safeguards**
   - AURA does not read third-party comments or messages, and never auto-replies.
   - Cards carry an owner-review queue only.
   - On return, the checklist is: review the official platform inbox yourself, import only official metrics, review stale drafts/rights/offer claims, then decide whether to continue a new coverage period.

## Product Image → Asset Reactor

A creator can provide a local/HTTPS/right-cleared product image reference, product description, factual core promise, CTA, rights confirmation and target lanes. The reactor builds a **local multi-format production package** immediately:

- six mini-picture / crop briefs: 1:1, 4:5, 9:16, 16:9, clean product cutout and detail/macro;
- five image-to-video treatments: product reveal, macro story, benefit demo, creator/UGC frame and short offer bumper;
- lane-specific copy drafts with hook, caption, CTA and disclosure reminder;
- product-page, compliant newsletter, paid-activation and comment-review playbooks;
- a human-gated local Social Growth handoff.

These are **render briefs and local drafts**, not falsely claimed images, videos, posts, newsletters, ads or sales results. A connected, authorized renderer and official platform/account are necessary for actual output and publication.

## Explicit boundaries

- No image-to-video renderer is connected by Area 97.
- No avatar is rendered and no voice is generated by Area 97.
- No audio is stored by AURA as part of the voice-consent record.
- No account is connected, no comment/DM read, no post sent, no email sent, no ad spend made, no payment collected and no product claim verified automatically.
- Every write needs `approved_by` and explicit `admin_authorization`.
- Own avatar use is rights-gated. Third-party likeness, name or voice authorization is never inferred.

## Operator workflow

1. In **AURA OS Launchpad**, create the local Access Profile and, if needed, a safe live-voice-consent record.
2. Open `/creator-continuity`, choose an intentionally conservative avatar/voice setting and define the personal boundary.
3. Add only original, owned or otherwise rights-cleared ideas/assets to the Content Bank.
4. Create the Time-Off Coverage plan with realistic cadence and selected lanes.
5. Review it, then activate it to preload local coverage cards before leaving.
6. Create Product Image packages for product launches; review each render/copy brief and hand it explicitly to Social Growth when appropriate.
7. If official connectors and a compliant local runner are implemented later, configure them as separate product modules and preserve a final human review gate.
8. After return, work through the recovery checklist and import only verified results.
